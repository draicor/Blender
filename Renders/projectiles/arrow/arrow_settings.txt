I resized the model until it fit correctly in the scene, I placed it under the character, and while in the aim animation, i posed it with the bow too, correctly holding it while aiming.
I then duplicated it, because we need one that is a child of the character, and one by itself with the same angles but as a stand alone projectile, i set the height to be 1.35m in Z so it flies from the hand of erika's character. I ended up moving the arrow 4° towards the screen when looking East, so it matches the bow animation.

I render 32 images, I start at East, at 0° (I set all transforms to 0° before starting) and keep adding -11.25° to Z until I do the 360° spin.

In Aseprite, I created 8 layers (E, SE, S, SW, W, NW, N, NE) each with 4 frames for the 360 spin. I centered all of the frames to the middle of the canvas and then resized it down from 256x256 to 96x96 so it fits nicely and uses less VRAM.

We could re use this for a fire arrow, but we would need to have an animation with fire at the tip, and possibly end up having a 4-8 frames animation for each of the 32 directions.

NOTE:
DON'T use shadows for projectiles, hide the plane from the render, its confusing, and if the projectile gets stuck to a target, like arrows do with destructibles, the shadow breaks immersion at some angles!
